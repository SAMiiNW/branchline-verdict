◉Branchline Verdict
SOURCE-BOUND DECISION ROUTING

The verdict is a destination, not a guess.

Branchline turns a written decision process into an on-chain binary tree. Validators read one complete public case record, answer only the questions reached by the evidence, and return the exact path. The contract follows the frozen YES/NO tracks and derives the terminal outcome itself.

1Place the route.
Freeze questions, branch targets, and allowed terminals.
2File a case.
Attach one public HTTPS record. It remains attributable by URL and digest.
3Walk once.
Consensus agrees on the path; skipped questions and invented outcomes are rejected.

Illustrative route: procurement preflight. The contract supports 2 to 8 reachable questions and 2 to 8 declared terminals.
